# GTM-PLAN — CV Builder SaaS

Full research backing: research-seo-gtm.md, research-market.md, research-competitors.md.

## Positioning
"Build a professional CV in 10 minutes — no design skills needed." Lead with **informality/underemployment**, not a single unemployment statistic (the honest picture per research is high informality, not simple joblessness — research-market.md §1). Differentiate on **MWK pricing + mobile-money checkout + Malawi-first template conventions**, since no researched competitor (global or regional) offers any of these.

## Launch Channel Priority (given $0 budget, solo dev)
1. **WhatsApp** — share into real, active Malawian job-seeker/graduate groups. Highest-leverage channel given confirmed WhatsApp-centric habits in the target persona set.
2. **Facebook groups** — same audience, similar tactic.
3. **University partnerships** — direct outreach to career centers/student unions (UNIMA, MUBAS, Mzuzu, etc.) offering free access for graduating classes. Higher effort, more durable (recurring cohorts).
4. **NGO partnerships** — the AfDB-funded "Jobs for Youth" (J4Y) program and similar youth-employment initiatives are a credible, mission-aligned partnership target.
5. Lower-priority/deferred until bandwidth allows: TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, Twitter/X (see research-seo-gtm.md §2 for full channel table).

## Content Plan
- 20 blog posts targeting Malawi-specific long-tail SEO keywords (full list in research-seo-gtm.md §3) — e.g., "How to Write a CV for Your First Job in Malawi," "CV Format for NGO Jobs in Malawi."
- A free, no-signup downloadable basic template as a lead magnet.
- English-first content; Chichewa tested selectively, not committed to at launch (see PRD Non-Goals).

## Pricing & Launch Offer
- **One-time payment per CV profile** (not a subscription, not a per-download charge) that unlocks unlimited clean re-exports of that CV — matches PayChangu's lack of native recurring billing, the segment's subscription-wary profile, and near-zero marginal cost per export (full rationale and comparison against every major competitor's "cheap trial → auto-renewing subscription" trap: research-business-model.md).
- "Founding user" discounted launch price for the first cohort to drive urgency — since marginal cost is near-zero, pricing low to build volume/trust costs little margin (research-business-model.md §4).
- Exact MWK price point: validate against real signups, not fixed in advance — willingness-to-pay data for this market wasn't found in research (flagged gap).
- Free tier: full build + preview + both templates + a watermarked export, so free users still get real, complete value; payment gates only the clean, watermark-free re-exportable version.
- **No data monetization** (no selling/sharing CV data with third parties) — stated explicitly as a differentiator, directly countering the data-broker practices found at global competitors (research-business-model.md §1, §5).

## Referral & Virality
- Low natural virality expected (a CV builder isn't inherently a shareable moment) — don't over-invest.
- Light referral incentive (extra export or small discount) layered onto the already-social WhatsApp discovery pattern.
- Discreet watermark on free exports as passive, non-annoying visibility.

## Metrics & Milestones
- **Week 1**: signups, CV completion rate.
- **Month 1**: signups vs. 100 target, free→paid conversion vs. 10% target, CV completion rate vs. 60% target (see PRD Success Metrics).
- **Month 3**: retention/return usage, channel attribution to decide where to double down.
- **North star**: completed, exported CVs.

## 90-Day Roadmap
| Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Pre-launch: finalize pricing, publish 3-5 seed blog posts, prep outreach list |
| 3-4 | Soft launch to a small seed group + one university career center; fix onboarding friction from direct feedback |
| 5-8 | Public launch across WhatsApp/Facebook groups; steady blog cadence; outreach to 2-3 more universities + one NGO program |
| 9-12 | Analyze metrics, double down on best channel, adjust founding-user price based on real conversion data, begin light TikTok/YouTube content if bandwidth allows |

## First 100 Users Playbook
See research-seo-gtm.md (final section) for the full step-by-step — summary: ship MVP → share honestly in 5-10 real WhatsApp/Facebook groups → pitch 2-3 university career centers → publish first 5 blog posts → talk directly to the first 10-20 signups to find friction before scaling further.
